About TNT Legacy Shield

Discipline built in the Marine Corps. Now put to work protecting your family.

TNT Legacy Shield was founded on a simple idea: the people who protect this country deserve the same level of protection for their own families, and so does everyone else.

Meet the Founder

Darrell Thompson

Darrell Thompson is a retired United States Marine Corps veteran and a retired federal government employee who has spent his life in service, first to his country, and now to the financial security of the families he works with.

He is a California licensed real estate agent, a multi-state licensed life, health, and Medicare insurance producer, and the author of Creating a Wealth Building Mindset. He founded TNT Legacy Shield to bring the same discipline, honesty, and mission-first mindset he learned in the Marine Corps to an industry that too often puts sales ahead of service.

USMC Veteran Retired Federal Employee CA Licensed Real Estate Agent Multi-State Insurance Producer Published Author

"I met my wife while we were both serving in the Marine Corps back in 1988. Her financial discipline changed the way I thought about money, and it's the same discipline I bring to every family I work with today."

That personal journey, from active duty, to marriage, to building real financial security for his own family, is what drives Darrell's mission at TNT Legacy Shield: helping families not just buy a policy, but build a lasting legacy. He's especially passionate about helping parents and grandparents put plans in place early for their children and grandchildren, so the next generation starts ahead instead of catching up.
